# Form & Field

![Form & Field programme](preview.png)

An approved GPT Image study for a fictional museum programme. Three events share one spatial scene rather than three separate UI cards.

## What carries the beauty

**The background is part of the information.** Stone, folded paper and a plant give the three themes a physical setting. A common floor, lighting and shadows join them into one composition.

- Cream, deep sage and terracotta form distinct fields; no single tint washes over everything.
- Dark text on the light field and pale text on the darker fields keep the programme readable within the scene.
- A large, high-contrast serif title anchors the composition. Smaller times and titles align across the three groups; widely spaced category labels create a quieter level.
- Text occupies calm upper areas; objects ground the lower area. Neither becomes an unrelated ornament beside the other.

## Reuse the relationships

Start with a short editorial programme, give each subject a meaningful visual counterpart, establish a shared light/ground, then reserve quiet text fields and align repeated information. Check the complete composition at its intended size before adding more content.

Useful for event programmes, cultural features or editorial introductions. Dense tables, long descriptions and narrow layouts need fresh composition tests.
