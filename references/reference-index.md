# Reference Index

## Included assets

### [Icon directions 01-18](../assets/icon-directions-01-18.jpeg)

A numbered icon exploration sheet. Its numbering identifies the directions used in sequential discussion.

Use it to:
- select an exact direction;
- compare structural diversity and visual distance;
- preserve each concept's metaphor, silhouette, and material language.

Do not use it to copy existing marks or average all directions into one style.

### [Icon detail sheet 01-06](../assets/icon-details-01-06.jpeg)

A six-icon sheet in two rows, corresponding to directions 01-06 in the numbered sheet at a larger scale.

Use it to inspect silhouette, negative space, composition, material, and lighting. Use the numbered sheet to identify the direction; do not substitute a neighboring icon's details.

## Selected visual examples

### [Curved gallery](../examples/gallery-001/README.md)

A dark three-panel gallery with a complete central panel and cropped neighbors.

Use it to study:
- asymmetric near/far curvature;
- focus through warm artwork inside a quiet black field;
- crop, continuation, and peripheral typography.

Do not treat it as a tested carousel or infer responsive behavior from the still image.

### [Orbital progress](../examples/progress-001/README.md)

A dark orbital composition with a large progress value, blue rim light, and a small warm marker.

Use it to study information scale, localized contrast, and visual rhythm between type and curves. Do not infer a calibrated progress arc or working loading state.

## Reference hierarchy

1. Actual task and content requirements.
2. Selected direction and its essential spatial relationships.
3. The reference's design thesis and applicable dimensions.
4. Platform and implementation constraints.

For a selected icon direction, preserve its thesis while maintaining recognition at intended sizes. For public production, check asset rights and create an original expression rather than copying protected identity.

See [Design sources](design-sources.md) for external discovery platforms and author resources.
