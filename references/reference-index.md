# Reference Index

Use this index for actual visual evidence. Use the [example scope index](../examples/README.md) to distinguish still studies, experimental systems and scoped implementations; use [design sources](design-sources.md) for the separate functional-reference search. An attractive retained image alone is not evidence of a reusable system.

## Included assets

### [Icon directions 01-18](../assets/icon-directions-01-18.jpeg)

A numbered icon exploration sheet. Its numbering identifies the directions used in sequential discussion.

Use it to:
- select an exact direction;
- compare structural diversity and visual distance;
- preserve each concept's metaphor, silhouette, and material language.

Do not use it to copy existing marks or average all directions into one style.

### [Icon detail sheet 01-06](../assets/icon-details-01-06.jpeg)

A six-icon sheet in two rows, corresponding to directions 01-06 in the numbered sheet at a larger scale.

Use it to inspect silhouette, negative space, composition, material, and lighting. Use the numbered sheet to identify the direction; do not substitute a neighboring icon's details.

## Selected visual examples

### [Curved gallery](../examples/gallery-001/README.md)

A dark three-panel gallery with a complete central panel and cropped neighbors.

Use it to study:
- asymmetric near/far curvature;
- focus through warm artwork inside a quiet black field;
- crop, continuation, and peripheral typography.

Do not treat it as a tested carousel or infer responsive behavior from the still image.

### [Orbital progress](../examples/progress-001/README.md)

A dark orbital composition with a large progress value, blue rim light, and a small warm marker.

Use it to study information scale, localized contrast, and visual rhythm between type and curves. Do not infer a calibrated progress arc or working loading state.

### [Amber clock](../examples/amber-clock/README.md)

A large warm-white time display in front of amber glass, with a compact cool-edged action.

Use it to study:
- material cues shared by artwork, type, and controls;
- a large dark object with concentrated edge brightness;
- asymmetric overlap and localized warm/cool contrast.

Do not treat the still image as a functioning timer or a verified type/color specification. The original verbatim generation prompt is unavailable.

### [Green ribbon](../examples/green-ribbon/README.md)

A stable black text block beside a flowing green ribbon and a small coral action.

Use it to study tactile context around typography, diagonal continuity, folds and shadows, and unequal color areas. Do not let the artwork obscure text or assume the plus symbol has tested behavior. The original verbatim generation prompt is unavailable.

### [Translucent UI example system](../examples/translucent-ui/README.md)

An approved selection-preview composition and a paired-choice composition. Use them together to study broad translucent surfaces, readable dark typography, numbered instruction pills, nested preview scale and restrained selected states. Retained PNGs preserve the whole and key details; implementation tokens are starting values, not a production system. The [Pastel Workspace page example](../examples/translucent-ui/pastel-workspace/README.md) adds a fictional English overview, desktop/narrow renderings, local-choice dialog and bounded runnable behavior.

The [selection frame](selection-frame.md) also has an independent guide for potential screenshot, crop and canvas use. Its appearance is approved in the original context; cross-background portability and complete interactions still require testing.

## Reference hierarchy

1. Actual task and content requirements.
2. Selected direction and its essential spatial relationships.
3. The reference's design thesis and applicable dimensions.
4. Platform and implementation constraints.

For a selected icon direction, preserve its thesis while maintaining recognition at intended sizes. For public production, check asset rights and create an original expression rather than copying protected identity.

See [Design sources](design-sources.md) for external discovery platforms and author resources.

## Material and surface studies

- [Material and attention](../examples/material-attention/README.md): equal-circle material comparisons and an outline-to-labeled-capsule attention progression. Use for geometry, transparency and edge emphasis; two related studies in one group.

Both include editable HTML/CSS and the scope of visual approval and outstanding technical checks. They are independent studies, not official Adobe components.
