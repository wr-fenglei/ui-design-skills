# Image Prompting for Design

## Match the prompt to the design stage

When producing the inspiration-led version, give GPT Image the shared brief: problem, usage context, content inventory, canvas and hard constraints, plus the inspiration and functional evidence gathered in the [method](method.md). Let it propose what carries the beauty. Do not paste another direction's layout, palette or draft into the prompt. Ask for a complete low-detail page at the comparison stage, as the [multi-direction workflow](../../references/multi-direction-workflow.md) requires. The relationship-specific guidance below applies after a core has been selected, or when faithfully studying a specific reference.

## Write relationships, not praise

Keywords: focal point, supporting mass, occupied area, overlap, quiet space.

Describe what leads, where it sits, how large it is relative to the canvas, and what remains quiet. Words such as "premium" or "beautiful" do not specify these relationships.

## Composition

- Define the canvas and intended use.
- State element placement, relative scale, crop, and overlap.
- Identify essential negative space.
- For refinement, list the relationships that must remain unchanged.

## Color roles

- Separate background, primary information, atmospheric subject, and action accent.
- Describe the amount and location of an accent, not just its name.
- Specify warm/cool and light/dark relationships before adding extra hues.

## Material and detail

- State the form and the lighting that reveals it.
- Describe where highlights, shadows, and texture belong.
- Keep broad surfaces quiet when edge light or curvature supplies the character.
- Avoid combining incompatible surface instructions without a clear purpose.

## Material relationships

- Describe how the visual subject and neighboring controls relate: shared light, temperature, softness, or contour.
- Keep that relationship selective. Do not give every element the same glass texture.
- Ask for restrained supporting elements that remain readable beside the expressive material.

## Text and controls

- Give exact wording when text is essential to the concept.
- Describe scale, alignment, and hierarchy rather than relying on a font name alone.
- Rebuild text and controls in code for functional UI; generated lettering is visual evidence only.

## Controlled edits

- Identify the target region and the requested change.
- State which composition, colors, and forms must stay fixed.
- Inspect the whole output for drift, including reflections and textures outside the target.
- Keep each comparison focused on a small number of variables.

## Evaluation

A good result does not establish which phrase caused it. Use controlled comparisons before claiming a prompting rule is reliable. Keep verbatim generation prompts distinct from later reconstructed briefs.
